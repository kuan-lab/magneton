## Technical Notes

### Why binary_watershed for mito?
- Waterz is designed for dense neuron segmentation (fills entire volume)
- Mitos are sparse objects floating in empty space
- Waterz creates supervoxels everywhere including background, resulting in large blobs
- `binary_watershed` only segments where probability exceeds threshold

### Algorithm (binary_watershed):
1. Seeds placed where `prob > 0.98` (confident mito centers)
2. Foreground mask where `prob > 0.85`
3. Watershed grows from seeds, bounded by mask
4. Empty space (prob < 0.85) remains unsegmented
