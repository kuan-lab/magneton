## Investigation: Merge Split Errors

**Problem:** Segmentation output showed "strips" at block boundaries where objects were connected within the strip but split from the rest of the volume.

**Diagnostic approach:** Given two segment IDs on either side of a split boundary, traced through the merge pipeline:
1. Checked `unions.txt` — confirmed the segments had zero union pairs (never matched)
2. Checked `global_offsets.json` — identified which blocks owned each segment
3. Read actual voxel data from both blocks in the overlap zone — computed overlap statistics

**Test case 1 (neuron, `fib_f_neuron_test`):** Segments 2 and 16648
- Block 0 (full volume) vs Block 1 (128-voxel edge block)
- 99.92% overlap (5.26M voxels), but `tot_gb=5.26M > max_voxel_size=1.5M` → filtered out

**Test case 2 (mito, `fib_f_mito_instances`):** Segments 4468 and 4684
- Blocks 24 and 25 — full-size blocks, middle of volume
- 100% perfect overlap (2.2M voxels, frac_local=1.0, frac_global=1.0)
- Still filtered: `tot_gb=2.2M > max_voxel_size=1.5M`

**Root cause:** The `max_voxel_size` check in `select_pairs()` (`relabel_utils.py:99`) rejects any pair where `tot_gb > max_voxel_size`. The 1.5M threshold was calibrated for lower-resolution data. At 4nm resolution, even moderate-sized mitos exceed it.
